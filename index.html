HOW TO PLAY

Mental Equity = your health. Keep it above zero.
Resistance = boss health. Bring it to zero.
Choose a move, then the boss takes a turn.

YOUR MOVES

EXECUTE Reliable attack.
RESET Restore your health.
WAIT Double your next attack's damage.
SNIPE Risk a weak hit for a powerful one.

You'll also have three special tools. Tips will help you through your first fight.
